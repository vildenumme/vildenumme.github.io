About

A little more personal than my LinkedIn profile.

Hi, I'm Vilde.

I'm pretty sure my path into cybersecurity was written in the stars back when I spent half my youth at LAN parties, building computers and ignoring bedtimes to play games. What started as a genuine passion eventually turned into a full-time career in IT.

Outside of tech, my life revolves around a few simple pleasures: Good books (ranging from terrifying monsters to hopelessly romantic main characters), video games, and spending a completely reasonable amount of time at home.

I love stories in general, which is why I have a lot of tattoos. They've turned my body into a living storybook, and I like to think of them as a visual timeline of everything I've loved throughout my life.

I live for autumn. The second the leaves change, the candles are lit and the horror marathons begins. I firmly believe Halloween deserves at least three months of celebration.
