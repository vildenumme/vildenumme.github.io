Building a Safe Phishing Lab

A controlled look at SMTP, spoofing, headers, and packet captures.

Jun 18, 20267 min readEmail security

This lab was built to understand how spoofed email appears at different stages—from the SMTP conversation to the final message headers.

Keeping the lab controlled

The entire setup stayed inside a local environment. The goal was analysis and learning, not delivering messages to real recipients.

MAIL FROM:<spoofed@example.com>
RCPT TO:<student@lab.local>
DATA

What I looked for

I compared visible sender information with the underlying headers and captured the SMTP traffic in Wireshark.
