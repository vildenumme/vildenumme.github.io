How I Structure Threat Intelligence Notes

Turning scattered indicators into useful context and readable reporting.

May 10, 20265 min readThreat intelligence

An indicator without context is just a piece of data. I wanted a structure that made it easier to connect technical findings with what they might mean.

My basic structure

  • What was observed?
  • Where did the information come from?
  • What context supports the assessment?
  • What remains uncertain?

Separate facts from assessment

Keeping observations, assumptions, and conclusions distinct makes the final report more trustworthy and easier to update.
